# Copilot Instructions

## Design Guide

- Keep the UI creative, polished, and modern, with a strong visual personality.
- Favor dark, moody surfaces with bright accent details and subtle glassmorphism.
- Use clear hierarchy: bold headings, concise supports, and distinct action controls.
- Make interactions feel tactile: generous spacing, rounded cards, soft shadows, and subtle hover transitions.
- Preserve accessibility and readability: high contrast text, responsive layouts, and simple semantic HTML.
- Lean into the social bingo theme with playful language, warm status indicators, and visible progress cues.
- Avoid generic or plain styling; the design should feel crafted and memorable while staying easy to scan.
